  ema : 102 tensors in best, 102 in latest, 102 shared,
        0 only-in-best, 0 only-in-latest
        DIFFER: 0 of 102

  G   : 108 tensors in best, 108 in latest, 108 shared,
        0 only-in-best, 0 only-in-latest
        DIFFER: 2 of 108
          attention.query.weight_u   max_abs_diff=0.00014403462409973145
          attention.query.weight_v   max_abs_diff=0.00014406442642211914

  D   : 51 tensors in best, 51 in latest, 51 shared,
        0 only-in-best, 0 only-in-latest
        DIFFER: 49 of 51
        identical: 2 of 51 -> ['sobel_x', 'sobel_y']

All three expected counts confirmed exactly: 0/102, 2/108, 49/51.

The two G tensors that differ are the spectral-norm power-iteration
buffers, which drift on any forward pass without an optimiser step -
they are not learned weights. Supporting detail worth having: the only
two D tensors that do NOT change are sobel_x and sobel_y, fixed
edge-detection kernels. They are constants, so their invariance
corroborates the reading rather than weakening it.

Mechanism (leading hypothesis, NOT proven): torch.amp GradScaler
silently skips the optimiser step when gradients hold inf/NaN, so
losses keep being computed and logged while G never moves. The scaler
state is not checkpointed, so this cannot be verified retroactively.
A guard that counts skipped steps per epoch and prints
"G DID NOT TRAIN" now exists in gan/gan_train.py (commit d085510).
