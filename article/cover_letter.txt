Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript "Generating Diverse Synthetic Eyeglass Datasets Using
GANs" for consideration as a research article in Electronics.

Readers of Electronics who build vision systems from small, domain-specific
image collections often turn to GAN-generated data, and they usually judge
the result by FID. Our manuscript shows, on a catalogue of 6,511 eyeglass
frame images, that FID does not reveal where such a generator loses
diversity, and it gives measures that do. Colour augmentation removed the
chromatic frames from the output, a mirror prior in the generator reduced the
variety of viewing angles to 0.54 of the real value, and the checkpoint with
the lowest KID was not the most diverse one in six of sixteen runs. None of
these effects was visible in FID.

The manuscript contributes a metric suite for this product (colour coverage,
pose-aligned symmetry, mirror-axis offset dispersion, a calibrated novelty
rate and structural artifact checks, each validated on synthetic input and
read against a measured noise floor), an ablation of eighteen training
configurations, and a comparison with the official StyleGAN2-ADA under the
same evaluation. The work continues our earlier publications on eyeglasses
detection in Electronics (2024, 13, 4763; 2025, 14, 2796).

The code, the trained generators and two generated datasets of 10,000 images
are public (GitHub and Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23287084).

The manuscript is original, has not been published before and is not under
consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission.
